Unit economics

Break-even by channel (dine-in & delivery)

I split sales and direct costs by channel: dine-in contribution vs delivery (including platform fees and packaging).

Fix rent, base payroll and other fixed costs. Then calculate the point where channel contribution covers the fixed costs (break-even).

Common outcome: delivery is profitable if you optimize AOV, product cost and timings—without cannibalizing dine-in.
