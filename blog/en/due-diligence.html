Due diligence

120+ checks before signing a lease

Before signing a lease, I validate 120+ points. Key blocks: extraction and air ducts, electrical capacity, water/drainage, soundproofing/neighbour noise, access and local regulations.

Ask for plans, certificates and previous permits. Check the community: bylaws and minutes, potential restrictions for hospitality. Consider opening hours and waste impact.

Negotiate using unit economics: target rent, capex, forecast sales by channel (dine-in/delivery) and expected payback.
